Your guide to Demandra

Hi, I am Demi, your guide. Pick your path and I will walk you through it in a few minutes.

  1. 1

    Create a free account

    Sign up with your college email. We never see your card or UPI PIN.

  2. 2

    Browse Discover

    Look for the green ✓ Validated tag and the % of target. Use search and category filters.

  3. 3

    Open an idea and check the safeguards

    Every campaign shows its verification checklist: founder ID, vendor SLA, approved sample and delivery date.

  4. 4

    Choose how strongly you want it

    Validate demand (10 XP), Will buy if it comes (25 XP) or Pre-commit (50 XP). You pay ₹0 today.

  5. 5

    Wait for the target

    If pre-commits reach 110% of the minimum, payments move to escrow on the end date. If not, mandates cancel and nobody pays.

  6. 6

    Collect and scan

    Pick up on campus and scan the QR code. That releases the founder’s final 60%.

  7. 7

    Level up

    Earn XP and badges for every idea you back. Check My rewards and share your impact.

  1. 1

    Sign up and open Launch

    Add your product, early-bird price and the minimum order target.

  2. 2

    Get verified

    We check your college ID, your vendor’s signed SLA and your product sample.

  3. 3

    Share in your class groups

    Every campaign has a WhatsApp share link. Early momentum matters.

  4. 4

    Hit 110% of your minimum

    That buffer covers failed debits, so a funded campaign stays funded.

  5. 5

    Submit your Udyam certificate

    Free to self-file, or ₹499 for our assisted filing. 40% is released against vendor invoices.

  6. 6

    Deliver with QR

    Backers scan at pickup and the final 60% is released.

  7. 7

    Go further

    Validated ventures are referred to incubators, EDII-TN cells and brand sandbox.

Words you will see

Pre-commit+

A conditional order. You approve a UPI AutoPay mandate but pay nothing unless the campaign is funded.

110% rule+

A campaign is funded only when pre-commits reach 110% of the minimum, to cover failed debits.

Escrow+

Funds are held in a nodal escrow account via Razorpay, not by Demandra or the founder.

Udyam / MSME+

Government registration that turns a campus project into a registered business.

Validated+

An idea that reached its target. It unlocks the next steps for the founder.

XP and badges+

Points and achievements you earn for backing ideas. They show your campus impact.
